04

Questions everyone asks

What does a film really cost?+

The entry point is the Launch Set: three ads for one product, in all three formats, with one revision round, for £595. Larger packages are listed in the pricing section.

How fast is it really?+

48 working hours from the moment the brief and product material are complete. The first half goes into the concept and agreeing it with you, the second into production. From your approval it's 24 hours to delivery. The revision round follows and usually takes another working day.

Who owns the rights?+

You get an unrestricted, perpetual, worldwide licence to use the delivered videos for web, social media and paid advertising. There are no further licence fees. I transfer every right I hold in the production. Because parts of the production are AI-assisted, the copyright status of such content is not settled consistently across jurisdictions. Nobody can honestly guarantee you exclusive protection against third parties.

What if I don't like the film?+

The Launch Set includes one revision round, the Creative Set two. They cost nothing. Further rounds are £130 each.

Do you work with AI?+

Yes. Production is AI-assisted, which is what makes 48 hours and fair prices possible. Concept, visual language and edit are still done by hand, and every frame is checked before it reaches you.

Tell me about your product.

Send me two or three sentences and a photo. Within a day you'll get an honest assessment and a fixed price.
